CSV Import Instructions

Upload a CSV file to import multiple customers at once.

Required columns:
  • name - Customer's full name
  • address - Complete address
  • mobile1 - Primary contact number
  • location - Area/district
Optional columns:
  • mobile2 - Secondary contact number
  • id_number - Customer identification number
  • initial_balance - Initial outstanding balance (default: 0)
  • customer_type - Customer category (B2C_READY_CASH, B2C_EMI, or B2B; default: B2C_READY_CASH)
  • collection_frequency - Schedule for collections (NONE, WEEKLY, or MONTHLY; default: NONE)
  • collection_day_of_week - Day for weekly collections (0-6, where 0 = Monday)
  • collection_day_of_month - Day for monthly collections (1-31)
